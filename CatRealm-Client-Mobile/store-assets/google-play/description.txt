CatRealm is a chat and community app. Join Realms with text channels, voice channels and watch-together theater rooms, or talk privately in end-to-end encrypted DMs and group chats.

Features:
- Servers, channels, threads, forums, reactions, emotes and stickers
- Encrypted direct messages and group chats, with voice messages
- Voice channels and DM calls
- Theater: watch videos together in a voice channel
- Push notifications, with per-server and per-conversation mutes
- Discover Servers, to find public communities
- Private notes for your own use
- A guided tour for new accounts

Sign in with a CatRealm Central account, or connect to a self-hosted CatRealm server with its address.
